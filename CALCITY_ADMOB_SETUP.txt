CALCITY ADMOB SETUP GUIDE (2026-08-08)
======================================

The app code is DONE: a banner ad now sits above the bottom nav bar on
every tab, and an interstitial shows every 3rd tab switch (max 1 per 2
minutes). Everything currently uses Google's OFFICIAL TEST ad IDs, so
the app works and shows fake "test ad" banners right now.

These are the steps only YOU can do — they need your Google account.

WHY ADS AREN'T SHOWING REAL ADS YET:
  - Test IDs are hardcoded as the default (safe for development).
  - Real ads need: an approved AdMob account + your App ID in the
    manifest + your ad unit IDs passed at build time.

─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
STEP 1 — Create the AdMob account
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  a) Go to https://apps.admob.com with your Google account
     (use mmsantelopevalley@gmail.com)
  b) Click "Sign up for AdMob" -> fill in your details -> submit
  c) You'll need to verify your phone number and address.
     Approval usually takes a few hours to a few days.

STEP 2 — Register the app
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  a) In AdMob: Apps -> Add app
  b) Platform: Android
  c) App name:  Cal City
  d) Package name (EXACT):  com.calcity.calcity_app
  e) App store listing: choose "No" (app is not on Google Play — you
     distribute via GitHub Releases). AdMob will ask about this —
     pick the option for an app not listed in a store.
  f) Create.

  IMPORTANT (policy heads-up): AdMob generally expects apps that show
  ads to be available in a store (Play/App Store). Ads MAY be served
  to sideloaded APK installs, but Google can pause serving if they
  determine the app isn't properly listed. If revenue matters, the
  safest long-term move is an UNLISTED Google Play listing (paid
  $25 one-time developer fee) purely to satisfy policy while still
  distributing via GitHub Releases.

STEP 3 — Get your App ID and ad unit IDs
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  a) AdMob -> Apps -> your app -> App settings -> scroll to "App ID".
     It looks like:  ca-app-pub-1234567890123456~1234567890
  b) Ad units -> Add ad unit -> Banner -> Create:
       Name: CalCity Banner
       Format: Banner (320x50)
     Copy the Ad unit ID:  ca-app-pub-1234567890123456/1234567890
  c) Ad units -> Add ad unit -> Interstitial -> Create:
       Name: CalCity Interstitial
     Copy the Ad unit ID:  ca-app-pub-1234567890123456/0987654321

STEP 4 — Wire the IDs into the app (on your machine)
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  a) Put your App ID in the manifest (replace the test ID):
     File:  calcity_app/android/app/src/main/AndroidManifest.xml
     Line:  <meta-data
              android:name="com.google.android.gms.ads.APPLICATION_ID"
              android:value="ca-app-pub-REAL-APP-ID"/>
  b) Build the release APK WITH your ad unit IDs:

     flutter build apk --release \
       --dart-define=ADMOB_BANNER_ID=ca-app-pub-1234567890123456/1234567890 \
       --dart-define=ADMOB_INTERSTITIAL_ID=ca-app-pub-1234567890123456/0987654321

     (Without the --dart-define flags, the APK uses TEST ads — safe,
     but you earn nothing.)

  c) Distribute the new APK via GitHub Releases as usual.

STEP 5 — Test on your phone BEFORE going wide
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  a) Install the test-ID APK (current build): you should see a gray
     "Test Ad" banner above the nav bar.
  b) Switch tabs 3 times: a test interstitial should appear.
  c) Only after AdMob approves your account, build with the real IDs
     and confirm real ads load (logcat shows "Ad served" lines).
